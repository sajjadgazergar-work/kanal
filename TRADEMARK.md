# Trademark policy

The KANAL name and mark are held separately from the copyright and are **not**
licensed by the AGPL license. This policy is modelled on the widely-used Rust
and Kubernetes formulations (plan §19.2). Questions that are not answered here
get a reply within **10 business days** — this is not a silent veto.

## Permitted without asking

- "Built with KANAL"
- "KANAL-compatible"
- Unmodified redistribution of the project
- Community packs named `kanal-pack-<something>`
- Referring to the project by name in prose, reviews, articles, talks

## Requires written permission

- Naming a hosted service or product "KANAL" or a confusingly similar variant
- Distributing a modified build under the KANAL name

## How to ask

Open an issue tagged `trademark` (public — the answer should be public too) or
email the address in `SECURITY.md`. Include what you are building and the
exact mark you want to use. Responses within 10 business days.

## Enforcement posture

The point of this policy is that "KANAL" means this project, not a category.
A confusingly similar name on a competing hosted service is the case we will
enforce; a blog post using the name in a review is not.
